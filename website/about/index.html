Our story

Spa-night glow, at home.

LumiMask started with a simple idea: a calming self-care moment shouldn't need a salon booking.

We wanted something light and comfortable you could wear while reading, relaxing or winding down — ten quiet minutes, a few evenings a week, that feel like a little spa night of your own.

Every LumiMask is chosen with care and shipped to customers in the United States, Spain, France, the United Kingdom and Canada. Thank you for making it part of your ritual.
